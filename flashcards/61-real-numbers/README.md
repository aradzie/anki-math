# Real Numbers

The axiomatic foundations of \( \mathbb{R} \) itself: the field axioms, the order axioms, and the completeness (least-upper-bound) axiom, along with their direct consequences — upper/lower bounds, supremum and infimum, the Archimedean property, and density of \( \mathbb{Q} \) and the irrationals in \( \mathbb{R} \).

Calculus proper — limits, differentiation, integration, sequences and series — lives in [flashcards/21-calculus/](../21-calculus/) and builds on this material rather than repeating it.
