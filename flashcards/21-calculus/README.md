# Calculus

Single-variable calculus, done rigorously: \( \varepsilon \)-\( \delta \) limits and continuity, differentiation, integration, and sequences and series.

This assumes the real number system as given — the properties of \( \mathbb{R} \) itself (completeness, supremum/infimum, the Archimedean property) live in [flashcards/61-real-numbers/](../61-real-numbers/), not here.

- [01-identities/](01-identities/) — formula recall decks (limits, derivatives, integrals, Taylor series, Laplace transforms), including cloze variants.
- [02-limits/](02-limits/) — the \( \varepsilon \)-\( \delta \) definition of a limit, limit laws, continuity.
- [03-differentiation/](03-differentiation/) — derivatives, the mean value theorems (Rolle's, MVT), differentiability tests, L'Hôpital's rule, inverse function differentiation.
- [04-integration/](04-integration/) — the Riemann integral, improper integrals, principal values, the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, integration of inverse functions.
- [05-seq-ser/](05-seq-ser/) — sequences, series and convergence tests, power series, Taylor series.
